You're exactly right on both points!

1. **Does it only support Anthropic?** 
Actually, no! `t3code` supports *both* Anthropic (via `@anthropic-ai/claude-agent-sdk`) AND OpenAI/GitHub Copilot (via a custom `CodexAdapter`). They abstracted both LLMs behind an elegant `ProviderRegistry` interface that emits a unified stream of `OrchestrationEvents` (like `command_execution`, `assistant_text`, etc). This means our GUI will seamlessly support any CLI agent we plug into it.

2. **No need to persist chats to the cloud (Convex)**
Absolutely. Since we just ripped the `aiConversations` tables out of Convex, we will implement their privacy-first architecture *exactly*. All chat history (Threads, Turns, Messages, Tool Calls) will be persisted completely locally inside an Electron JSON/SQLite store that syncs directly to our React `Zustand` store via IPC streams.
