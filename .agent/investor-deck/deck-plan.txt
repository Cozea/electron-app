Communication job

By the end, early-stage software and AI investors should understand that Cozea can become both the provider-neutral team control plane for agentic development and the organization-wide distribution layer for internal DevApps, believe that a functioning product creates a credible wedge, and see a disciplined first-round plan to prove repeatable team adoption.

Audience

Pre-seed and seed investors evaluating AI developer tools, collaboration infrastructure, and enterprise software.

Purpose

Detailed investor read deck. It must stand alone without a live presenter, distinguish current product evidence from proposed strategy, and avoid invented traction, revenue, or customer claims.

Narrative arc

1. Agent capability is rising faster than team coordination.
2. The market validates demand, but incumbent products optimize agents or individual developers.
3. Cozea supplies the shared operating layer across people, providers, and tools.
4. Product architecture, programmable workflows, and organization-wide DevApp distribution create defensibility.
5. Free adoption plus team and enterprise controls create monetization.
6. A focused first round funds commercial proof, not speculative scale.

Visual route

Codex Grid composition reference: white canvas, strong black typography, pale gray structural panels, thin rules, restrained light-blue accent, authentic Cozea screenshots as the dominant visual evidence. Candidate silhouettes: slide-08 image split, slide-10 text/checklist, slide-17 timeline, slide-19 metric-led. Adjacent slides vary between image-led, narrative, comparison, timeline, table, and metric compositions.

Fundraising recommendation

Founder-selected first-round ask: $1M for 10% ownership, equivalent to $10M post-money economics. The round is framed as a focused 15–18 month proof window for retention, paid adoption, and team expansion; final instrument and closing mechanics require counsel.
